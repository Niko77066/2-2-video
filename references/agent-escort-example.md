# Agent escort pattern — adapted worked example

Use this only as a structural example. Replace the story details for the user's project.

## What the source prompt does well

- Define each character by appearance, movement quality, fighting specialty, and narrative function.
- Define the object with production-level material and interior details.
- Lock the train geography before choreographing action.
- Use short timestamped causal beats: surveillance, trigger, interception, escalation, reset, reveal.
- Put SFX directly under the visual event they synchronize with.

## 15-second action rhythm

```text
0:00-0:03 — establish protagonists, object, and two observers; use inserts to reveal surveillance
0:03-0:04 — brief calm and environmental motion
0:04-0:05 — theft attempt triggers the action
0:05-0:06 — simultaneous counteractions establish each protagonist's specialty
0:06-0:09 — escalation with clear physical cause and effect
0:09-0:12 — return to the original spatial composition and show the outcome
0:12-0:15 — object reveal and emotional release; finish on a locked product frame
```

## 音乐化提示 → 物理声

模型不生成音乐，所以源 Prompt 里的拨弦、加速配乐、爵士鼓、音乐骤停和合唱爆发全部要换成可见来源的物理声；BGM 在硬切合成后作为单独音轨加入。

Replace the source prompt's plucked strings, accelerating music, jazz drums, music cut, and choir burst with visible-source audio:

```text
0:00-0:03 SFX: train ambience, rail-joint clatter, newspaper rustle, pocket-watch tick and soft metal click
0:03-0:04 SFX: carriage vibration, cup-and-saucer tremor, window rattle
0:04-0:05 SFX: seat scrape, fast shoes in the aisle, leather handle pull, brass hardware strain
0:05-0:06 SFX: newspaper slap, rapid footsteps, cloth movement, muffled jaw impact, wrist grip, case thump
0:06-0:09 SFX: shoe pivots, sleeve snap, two restrained body impacts, wood resonance, heavy breathing
0:09-0:12 SFX: action sounds stop; only train ambience, cloth adjustment, controlled breathing
0:12-0:15 SFX: brass locks click open, hinge movement, padding creak, tiny fruit movement, quiet relieved exhale
```

## Boundary correction

Do not end on a vague emotional beat. End on a reusable composition:

```text
0:12-0:15 — HARD-CUT END FRAME
Stable extreme close-up POV from inside the cream-lined case, 24mm feel. Blueberries fill the lower foreground in precise rows; the male and female agents lean over the opening in the upper third, faces stable and relaxed; brass rim forms a clean frame around them. Camera fully locked. The last blueberry stops moving, both gazes settle on the center row, then hold the exact composition for 0.5 seconds. No fade, dissolve, morph, or camera drift.
SFX: final brass hinge click, one tiny blueberry settling, train ambience only.

NEXT SEGMENT CUT HANDLE
Hard cut to a wide exterior or overhead compartment shot while preserving the centered case axis or blueberry-blue color accent.
```
